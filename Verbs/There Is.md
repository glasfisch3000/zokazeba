# "There is…"

The phrase "there is/are" doesn't translate literally to zokazeba, but rather is an exception to the language itself.
As it is essentially a conjugated verb ("to be") without a real subject, zokazeba has a special form of the verb *zhi* that represents exactly that:

 - present-tense: *zhe* - "there is" (subjunctive: *ame zhi*)
 - past-tense: *zhire* - "there was" (subjunctive: *amire zhi*)
 - future-tense: *ze zhi* - "there will be" (subjunctive: *ame zi zhi*)
